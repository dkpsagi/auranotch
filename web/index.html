Frequently Asked Questions

Does AuraNotch work on Macs without a physical notch? +

Yes! If your MacBook Pro, MacBook Air, Mac mini, Mac Studio, or iMac doesn't have a physical camera notch, AuraNotch renders a synthetic rounded floating Dynamic Island at the top of your screen. You get 100% of the functionality regardless of your Mac hardware.

Where does my clipboard, notes, and file data go? +

Nowhere. Everything is stored locally on your Mac. AuraNotch has zero cloud servers, zero analytics trackers, and zero telemetry. Sensitive password-manager entries (such as 1Password, Bitwarden, and macOS Keychain) are strictly filtered and never recorded.

How does license activation work? +

Immediately after completing checkout, you will receive an Ed25519-signed license key (e.g. AURA-XXXX-XXXX). Enter this key once inside AuraNotch Settings. It is stored securely in your macOS Keychain and verifies cryptographically without requiring constant internet access.
